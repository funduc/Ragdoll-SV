# Vault Run — first campaign guide

Ten main levels lead to the Santor Gauntlet. Bronze opens the next level; the best medal and optional Santor Medal are saved separately for each character. Overtime is an optional harder rematch.

Budget: about **24 minutes including introductions, practice and retries**, not forced waiting. Human first-play pacing has not been measured. Each jump retains the existing 20-second simulation limit.

A successful landing is Clean or Scrappy without a crash. A controlled landing is Clean. Good-or-better pushes/braces include Perfect. Each level has an explicit Begin jump confirmation.

## 1. ORIENTATION DAY

Find the rhythm, then spend one well-timed push at the ramp edge.

**Objective:** Teach rhythmic acceleration and takeoff timing.

**Condition:** Original seeded lesson condition; shown before play. **Practice budget:** 2 minutes. **Upgrade reward:** one choice after first Bronze this run.

| Medal | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Bronze | Reach the ramp and finish the attempt. |
| Silver | Finish with at least 2 Good-or-better pushes. |
| Gold | Finish with a Perfect takeoff. |
| Santor (optional) | Perfect takeoff, no Missed pushes and 2 Good-or-better pushes. |

**John’s introduction:** “Welcome to orientation. Release the key between pushes.”

- **Jake:** “Jake: slower rotation, steady delivery. Learn the beat first.”
- **Brandon:** “Brandon: save the vocabulary for the results. Tap on the beat.”

## 10. THE SANTOR GAUNTLET

Three short jumps. Three announced conditions. Each result is banked; all three ordinary attempt totals add to one Gauntlet score. Leaving or refreshing restarts the three-heat sequence.

**Objective:** Use rhythm, takeoff, tricks and bracing across all three heats. Complete Bronze to finish the campaign and unlock the harder Gauntlet.

**Condition:** Three announced heats (below). **Practice budget:** 4 minutes. **Upgrade reward:** none.

| Medal | Requirement |
| --- | --- |
| Bronze | Complete all 3 jumps and bank at least 900 combined points. |
| Silver | Bank 1,800 points, 2 successful landings, 1 Good-or-better Brace and 1 unique trick. |
| Gold | Bank 2,400 points, 3 successful landings, 2 Good-or-better Braces and 2 unique tricks. |
| Santor (optional) | 3,200 combined points, 3 Clean landings and 3 Perfect Braces. |

**John’s introduction:** “This is the final examination. I have misplaced the marking scheme.”

- **Jake:** “Jake has three attempts to demonstrate a measurable reaction.”
- **Brandon:** “Brandon, three acts. A successful conclusion is permitted this time.”
- **Owen:** “Owen's final service includes three jumps and no scheduled wheel replacement.”

**Success:** “CAMPAIGN COMPLETE. THE HARDER GAUNTLET HAS ESCAPED CONTAINMENT.”

**Failure:** “All three scores are banked. Review the requirements, then demand a rematch.”

| Heat | Condition | John Santor |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. SPEED | Boost Strip | Heat one. Find your rhythm. This remains a supervised sporting event. |
| 2. CONTROL | Icy Ramp | Heat two. Brace on ice. SUPERVISION IS NOW A THEORETICAL CONCEPT. |
| 3. COMMITMENT | Wrate Issue | FINAL HEAT. THE WARNING IS SCHEDULED. MY RESPONSE IS NOT. |

Each heat banks its ordinary distance + style + landing + attachment score. The three totals add exactly; trick variety is the union across heats. Medals are awarded only after heat three. Each next heat has its own Ready screen. Leaving or refreshing restarts the heat sequence; previously saved best medals remain.
